# Renting a flat

## Documents for a rental application
- Passport or EU ID card
- Portuguese tax number (NIF); we can help you get one in about a week
- Proof of income: last 3 payslips, an employment contract, or for freelancers the last tax return
- If you have no income in Portugal: a guarantor or 6 months of rent paid in advance

## Lease terms
The standard lease is 12 months and can be renewed. Shorter stays of 3-11 months are available on selected flats,
marked "flexible term" in the listing. After the first year, the tenant can leave with 120 days' notice.

## Pets
Pets are allowed only where the listing says "pets allowed". Some landlords ask for an extra deposit of one month's rent for pets.

## Timeline
From application to keys usually takes 5-10 working days: landlord approval, lease signing, deposit payment.
