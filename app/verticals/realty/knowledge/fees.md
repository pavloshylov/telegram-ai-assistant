# Fees and commission

## Rentals
Tenants pay no agency fee at Northwind Homes. The landlord pays our commission.
Before moving in, a tenant usually pays the first month of rent plus a security deposit of two months' rent.
The deposit is returned within 30 days after the end of the lease, minus documented damage.

## Purchases
For buyers our service is free; the seller pays a commission of 5% of the sale price plus VAT.
Buyers should budget for transfer tax (IMT), stamp duty of 0.8% and notary and registration costs of about 1,000-1,500 EUR.

## Other costs
Utilities (electricity, water, internet) are paid by the tenant unless the listing says "utilities included".
Building maintenance fees (condominium) are paid by the owner unless the listing says otherwise.
