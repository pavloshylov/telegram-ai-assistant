# Buying a property

## Steps
1. Viewing and offer: we submit your written offer to the seller.
2. Promissory contract (CPCV): both sides sign and the buyer usually pays a 10% deposit.
3. Final deed (escritura) at a notary, typically 30-60 days after the CPCV.

## Mortgages
Non-residents can get a mortgage in Portugal, usually up to 70% of the property value; residents up to 90%.
We work with independent mortgage brokers and can introduce you at no cost.

## Foreign buyers
There are no restrictions on foreigners buying property in Portugal. You need a NIF (tax number) and a Portuguese bank account.
