# Viewings

## Booking a viewing
Viewings are free and take about 30 minutes. You can book one in this chat: pick a flat and tap "Book a viewing",
or ask the assistant. An agent confirms the time by phone or Telegram within 2 working hours.
Viewings run Monday to Saturday, 9:00-19:00. Sunday viewings are possible for sale properties by arrangement.

## Video viewings
If you are not in Lisbon yet, we offer live video viewings over WhatsApp or Google Meet at no cost.
The agent walks through every room and answers questions live; the call can be recorded on request.

## What to bring
Bring an ID or passport. For rentals, having proof of income ready speeds up the application if you like the flat.

## Cancelling
Please cancel or move a viewing at least 3 hours in advance by replying in this chat or calling the office.
