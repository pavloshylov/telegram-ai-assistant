# Payment

## Methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Bank transfer is available for orders over 300 EUR.
Payment in 3 interest-free instalments is available with Klarna for orders from 150 EUR.

## Invoices
Companies can get a VAT invoice: add the company name and VAT number when placing the order or ask in this chat.

## Prices
All prices include VAT. Discount codes can be applied at checkout; only one code per order.
