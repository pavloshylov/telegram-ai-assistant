# About Northwind Audio

## Who we are
Northwind Audio is an online store for headphones, earbuds and portable speakers (demo company for this project).
We stock brands we have tested ourselves and keep the range small on purpose.

## Choosing headphones
- For travel and open offices: over-ear headphones with active noise cancelling (ANC).
- For sport: earbuds with an IP rating of IPX4 or higher and secure ear hooks or wings.
- For calls: models with a "multipoint" connection let you switch between laptop and phone.
