# Delivery

## Options and prices
- Courier delivery in the EU: 1-3 working days, 6.90 EUR. Free for orders over 99 EUR.
- Pickup point (DPD / InPost lockers): 2-4 working days, 3.90 EUR. Free for orders over 99 EUR.
- Express next-day delivery (order before 14:00, Monday-Friday): 14.90 EUR.
We currently ship to EU countries, the UK and Ukraine. Delivery to Ukraine takes 5-8 working days and costs 12 EUR.

## Tracking
You get a tracking link by email and in this chat as soon as the parcel leaves our warehouse.
Orders placed before 14:00 on working days are shipped the same day.
